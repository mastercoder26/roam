# Watkins recreation

Open [the local editor](http://127.0.0.1:8774/Editor.html) to play the video and edit the project. If it is no longer running, double-click `Start Editor.command` in this folder.

- `Watkins-Recreation.mp4` is the finished 1440 × 1080, 4:3 video at 24 fps.
- `Watkins-Recreation.tsrct` is the portable Tesseract project, with its audio, footage, and fonts included.
- `Previews` contains the filmstrip and side-by-side comparisons with the supplied video.
- `Versions` keeps the earlier recreation and backups made by the editor.

Choose a time, then select a scene or expand an icon. Edit text, color, position, size, rotation, or opacity; click **Apply changes**, **Save project**, then **Export video**. Moving an animated icon also moves its existing path. **Show all scenes** and the search box help find material outside the current frame. Advanced controls expose artwork paths and animation values. Ink trails are native shapes grouped by frame.

The revision includes measured icon turns, overlapping carousels, the fast diagonal turn, curved ink trails, diffuse grainy orbs, orange impacts, and the later blackening of the heart, skateboard, coin, and cash. It contains 2,755 native layers and 791 animation tracks. The exported video retains the original AAC audio packets; the editable project contains the same mix as lossless WAV. The editor preserves the original audio packets when the audio remains unchanged.

The human profile and hand performances use the supplied footage, with separate editable words over them. Graphic shapes, icons, lettering, textures, and motion are reconstructed and editable. Some artwork, ink shapes, perspective, and typography remain approximations rather than an exact reconstruction of the original authoring project.

The editor uses the installed Tesseract renderer. Keep the whole folder together to retain the local editor, original audio stream, and backups. The `.tsrct` file can be moved on its own to another compatible Tesseract installation.
